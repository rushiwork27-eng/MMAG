mmag.in redesign · site structure proposal

Show the work. Then ask for the call.

Buyers judge an agency by what it has made and what it moved. Today mmag.in hides 80+ portfolio images on unlinked pages and tells its 7 case studies in text only. The new site puts work up front, puts a result beside every visual, and leads every page to one action: book a call.

Conversion

One primary action everywhere

Today there are 3 forms, and both popups require 5 fields each. "Get in Touch" links nowhere, and the contact email is a staging address.

PrimaryBook a callHeader button, end of every page, mobile bar. Calendar first, form second.
SecondaryGet portfolio (PDF)For buyers who must forward it. 3 fields: name, work email, company.
UtilityWhatsApp · CallMobile sticky bar and Contact page. Needs a business number and someone to answer it.
GA4 key eventFires whenWhy
book_callBooking confirmed (thank-you state)Main success measure
portfolio_download/get-portfolio/thank-you/ loadsLead-capture volume
brief_submitContact form sentSecond route to a lead
whatsapp_click · phone_clickTap on mobile bar or ContactMobile intent (cannot see the chat itself)

Expected result: clearer paths should lift these events, but there is no baseline (no GA4 access yet). Record 4 weeks of the current site first, then compare. No target numbers are set until then.
